🛒 E-commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)


This project presents an interactive E-commerce Sales Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, product trends, and regional distribution.

The dashboard helps in understanding:

Sales and profit performance 📈
Product-level insights 🛍️
Customer segments 👥
Regional trends 🌍
Shipping behavior 🚚
📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)
YTD Sales: $2.06M
YTD Profit: $233.7K
YTD Quantity Sold: 18.9K
Profit Margin: 11.35%
🔍 Analysis Performed
1. 📦 Category-wise Sales Analysis
Office Supplies generated the highest sales
Furniture and Technology showed moderate growth
Some categories experienced negative YoY growth

 Insight: Focus more on high-performing categories and improve low-performing ones

2. 🏆 Top & Bottom Products Analysis
Identified Top 5 products contributing most to revenue
Identified Bottom 5 products with lowest sales



Top products drive major revenue → maintain inventory
Low-performing products may need promotion or removal
3. 🌍 Region-wise Sales Distribution
Sales are distributed across West, East, Central, and South regions
Certain regions contribute significantly more revenue

👉 Insight:

High-performing regions should be prioritized
Low-performing regions need marketing strategies
4. 🚚 Shipping Type Analysis
Most sales come from Standard Class shipping
Faster shipping modes contribute less

👉 Insight:

Customers prefer cost-effective delivery over speed
5. 🗺️ Geographic Analysis (Map View)
Visualized sales across different states
Identified high-demand locations

👉 Insight:

Focus marketing campaigns in high-sales areas
Expand operations in underperforming regions
6. 👥 Segment Analysis
Segments include:
Consumer
Corporate
Home Office

👉 Insight:

Consumer segment contributes the largest share
Corporate segment shows stable performance
💡 Key Business Insights
Sales are strong but profit margin can be improved
Some categories and products are underperforming
Majority customers prefer standard shipping
Regional imbalance exists in sales distribution
High dependency on a few top products
